About David

Hi, my name is David VanDyke, and I've lived in West Michigan my entire life. This community means the world to me, and I'm passionate about helping the people around me protect the things they love most.

Before transitioning to insurance, I spent 10 years in real estate, where I gained a deep understanding of the importance of safeguarding homes and investments. Today, I use that experience to provide tailored home and auto insurance solutions that offer peace of mind and security.

"Let's work together to protect what matters most to you."

When I'm not helping clients, I enjoy watching sports (Go Blue!), collecting fountain pens, and reading books beside a cozy fire. Most of all, I love spending time with my wife, Caroline, and our four wonderful children—Weston, August, Callum, and Charlotte. Frequently Asked Questions

What types of insurance do you offer?

I can help with auto, home, life, Medicare, business, and more. If you're not sure where your need fits, just ask and I'll point you in the right direction.

Do you only serve Michigan?

I'm based in West Michigan and work with many local families, but I'm licensed in AZ, FL, IL, IN, MI, MO, NC, OH, and UT. Coverage availability varies by state.

How do I get a quote?

Send me a quick note through the contact form. I'll follow up by email with next steps and any info I need to get accurate quotes.

What information will you need from me?

It depends on your situation, but I'll usually ask for basic driver details, vehicles, and your current address. For home coverage, I may ask about the property, roof age, and systems. I'll keep it simple and guide you through it.

Can you compare multiple carriers?

Yes. Shield is an independent agency, which means I can shop multiple carriers to find the right fit for your coverage and budget.

Do you handle business insurance?

Yes. I can help with business and contractor coverage as well as specialty needs like events and liability.

How fast will you respond?

I do my best to respond quickly, usually within one business day.

Why work with a local agent?

You get a real person who knows the area and will be there when you need help, especially if you have a claim.
