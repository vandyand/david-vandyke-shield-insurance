About David

Hi, my name is David VanDyke, and I've lived in West Michigan my entire life. This community means the world to me, and I'm passionate about helping the people around me protect the things they love most.

Before transitioning to insurance, I spent 10 years in real estate, where I gained a deep understanding of the importance of safeguarding homes and investments. Today, I use that experience to provide tailored home and auto insurance solutions that offer peace of mind and security.

"Let's work together to protect what matters most to you."

When I'm not helping clients, I enjoy watching sports (Go Blue!), collecting fountain pens, and reading books beside a cozy fire. Most of all, I love spending time with my wife, Caroline, and our four wonderful children—Weston, August, Callum, and Charlotte. Whether it's enjoying the beauty of West Michigan or cheering on my favorite team, I'm grateful to call this place home.

Shield is an independent agency with a long track record of serving Michigan families. I'm proud to be part of those strong client relationships, and I can compare options across a broad marketplace of carriers to match coverage and budgets with confidence.
